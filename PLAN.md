# Palm & Marina Realty: AI Phone Concierge

## The product

An AI phone concierge for a Dubai real estate brokerage, "Palm & Marina Realty" (fictional).
It answers every call, 24 hours a day, in English or Arabic, and handles the first part of a
human sales agent's job: understand what the caller wants, find suitable properties, explain
the costs, and book a viewing with an agent.

## The problem

- **Buyers call at all hours.** Dubai attracts investors from London, Moscow, Mumbai and
  Beijing. Their evening is the middle of the night in Dubai, and nobody answers.
- **Missed calls are lost deals.** A buyer who can't get through calls the next brokerage.
- **Agents waste time on unqualified calls,** repeating the same answers about prices, fees
  and visas instead of focusing on serious buyers.
- **Viewings get double-booked:** two clients sent to the same agent at the same time.

## Who uses it

| Who | How |
|---|---|
| Buyers, investors and tenants | Phone the agency and talk to the concierge |
| Sales agents | Receive qualified leads, booked viewings and hot-lead transfers |
| Marketing team | Switch on promotions when a new project launches |
| Management | See calls, leads and viewings each day |

## What it does

1. **Greets every caller professionally,** with the brokerage's compliance statement, word for word.
2. **Recognizes returning callers:** "Welcome back, James. Your viewing is on Saturday at 4."
3. **Knows where the caller is calling from,** and gives times in Dubai time and theirs.
4. **Understands what they want:** buy, rent, sell, ask about a property, ask about costs,
   manage a viewing, or speak to a person.
5. **Qualifies the buyer:** buying or renting, area, bedrooms, budget.
6. **Searches available properties** and describes the best matches.
7. **Explains the real cost of buying:** transfer fee, agency fee, down payment, and whether the
   price qualifies for a UAE Golden Visa, always followed by "these are estimates, not financial advice."
8. **Books property viewings** with the right agent, never double-booking, and confirms
   the details before booking.
9. **Lets callers check or cancel** their own viewings.
10. **Scores every lead.** Hot leads go straight to a human agent.
11. **Hands over to a human** when the caller asks, gets frustrated, or the call runs long.
    If no agent is free, it takes a callback request.
12. **Texts follow-ups:** a reminder before the viewing, and the brochure or floor plan during the call.
13. **Launch-day mode:** marketing flips one switch, and every caller hears about the new project first.
14. **Daily visibility for management,** plus quick alerts when something stops working.

## A typical caller

> **11pm Dubai time. James calls from London.**
> He hears the compliance statement, says he wants a 2-bedroom in Dubai Marina up to AED 2.5 million.
> The concierge finds matches, describes the best one, explains the total cost and that it
> qualifies for a Golden Visa, then the disclaimer. He books a viewing for Saturday at 4pm Dubai
> time (1pm London), hears the details read back, and gets the floor plan by text.
>
> **Friday:** a reminder text about the viewing.
>
> **Next week James calls again:** "Welcome back, James." He's now a hot lead, so the call goes
> straight to a human agent who already knows what he wants.

## Build in stages

Each stage should work end to end before starting the next.

| Stage | What callers get |
|---|---|
| **1. Basic conversation** | A professional greeting, the concierge understands what they want (buy, rent, sell, ask about a property) and closes the call politely |
| **2. Property search** | It finds real matches from our listings and describes them |
| **3. Knows the caller** | It recognizes returning callers and where they're calling from |
| **4. Costs and Golden Visa** | It explains buying costs, with the disclaimer |
| **5. Viewings** | It books viewings without double-booking, and callers can check or cancel them |
| **6. Hot leads and humans** | Serious buyers go straight to an agent; anyone can ask for a person |
| **7. Launch-day mode** | Marketing can switch on a launch promotion instantly |
| **8. Follow-ups** | Viewing reminders and brochures by text |
| **9. Management view** | Daily numbers and alerts when something breaks |

## What it does not do

It doesn't close sales, give financial or legal advice, negotiate prices, or replace agents.
It handles the first conversation and passes serious buyers to people.

The brokerage and listings are fictional for this project.
